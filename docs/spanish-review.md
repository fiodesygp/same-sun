# Spanish review: terms and texts to check

Same Sun is a game for players about 12–13 years old (Girl Scouts Beyond Bars × ASU). The Spanish uses friendly "tú". All Spanish text is in [`src/i18n/es.json`](../src/i18n/es.json); the English is in [`src/i18n/en.json`](../src/i18n/en.json) with the same keys.

Please check that each item below is natural for these players and for the program's region, and suggest a better choice where needed.

## New: the driving question, mission and tagline (Home tab)

| Where | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Hero tagline (`home.tagline`) | Little land. Little water. Lots of sun. You decide how to share it. | Poca tierra. Poca agua. Mucho sol. Tú decides cómo compartirlo. |
| Your mission (`home.mission`) | Build a micro-lab for one sunny desert spot. Make it grow food and power a fan, a light and a pump. Then show your family how it works. | Construye un microlaboratorio para un lugar soleado del desierto. Haz que produzca comida y que dé energía a un ventilador, una luz y una bomba. Luego muéstrale a tu familia cómo funciona. |
| Our big question (`home.bigQuestion`) | In a desert where land, water, and shade are hard to come by, how can you use STEM to build a micro-lab that shows your family how one sunny space can grow food and power other things at once? | En un desierto donde la tierra, el agua y la sombra son difíciles de conseguir, ¿cómo puedes usar STEM para construir un microlaboratorio que le muestre a tu familia cómo un mismo lugar soleado puede producir comida y dar energía a otras cosas al mismo tiempo? |

Questions for these three:
- **STEM:** keep the English acronym, or use "CTIM" (ciencia, tecnología, ingeniería y matemáticas)?
- **micro-lab:** is "microlaboratorio" clear, or is "mini laboratorio" better?
- **power (a fan…):** "dar energía a" — or "hacer funcionar", "alimentar"?
