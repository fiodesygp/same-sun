Grow food. Make energy. Share the same sun.

In Same Sun you build a mini agrivoltaic system: a small solar panel set above a plant, so one sunny spot makes energy and grows food. Test your ideas here, then try them on your real kit.

Our driving question

In a desert where land, water, and shade are hard to come by, how can you use STEM to build a micro-lab that shows your family how one sunny space can grow food and power other things at once?

What is a mini agrivoltaic system?

Panel above

A small solar panel turns sunlight into electricity that lights an LED or spins a tiny fan.

Plants below

Seedlings grow in the panel’s shade, which can shelter them from desert heat.

You design it

You choose panel size, height and plant, then test what works and why.

How to play

Three parts. Each one matches something you do with your real kit.

Design · Predict · Test · Explain

Can one sunny spot grow food and make energy?

Set up your panels above a seedling, guess what will happen, then run a sunny Arizona day and see how your plant and your panel did.
